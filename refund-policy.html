Course fees

Applicable course fees are displayed on the website or communicated before enrolment. Applicable taxes, if any, are extra and will be communicated before payment.

Once a student has enrolled and the course fee has been paid, the fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, except where a refund or other remedy is required under applicable law.

Batch changes

Requests for a batch change or adjustment should be made as early as possible. They will be considered by management subject to availability and applicable terms.

Course information

Students should review the syllabus, eligibility criteria, duration, schedule and fees before enrolment. Course details not displayed on the website should be confirmed with the academy before payment.
