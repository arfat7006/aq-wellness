About us

AQ Wellness & Aesthetics Centre

A welcoming wellness and therapy centre in Lar, Ganderbal, Jammu & Kashmir.

Our approach

AQ Wellness & Aesthetics Centre is focused on providing a welcoming environment for individuals seeking traditional and complementary wellness therapies. Our centre offers a range of services, including cupping therapy, massage therapy, acupressure, Sujok therapy, Panchakarma and other wellness practices.

As an Alternative Medicine Practitioner, our approach is centred on wellness, relaxation and individual care. We believe in explaining our services transparently and helping clients make informed choices.

Our therapies are offered for general wellness and relaxation purposes. We do not claim to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Our services are not a substitute for consultation with a qualified medical professional.

Our aim

To make wellness services accessible in a professional, respectful and comfortable environment.
