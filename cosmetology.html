Professional Beauty Courses

Course details and fees are presented by AQ Wellness & Aesthetics Centre. Training is conducted through V-Fit Wellness Academy.

Basic Beauty Course

₹34,999

Applicable taxes extra, if any.

Eligibility

Open to anyone interested in learning the basics of beauty treatments. No academic education requirement is specified.

Course syllabus

  • Basic manicure: Basic, French, Reverse French, Paraffin
  • Basic pedicure: Mini, Basic, French, Paraffin
  • Foot spa: Fruit, Chocolate
  • Face spa and facials: De-tan, Whitening, Anti-ageing
  • Threading and waxing: Cold, Hot
  • Head spa with pressure points on face and head
  • Head, neck and shoulder massage techniques
  • Basic full body massage technique
  • Basic makeup: Simple, Minimal Eyes, Smokey Eyes
  • Face clean-up and bleach: Whitening, Insta Glow
  • Revision and examination

Duration options

  • Weekend: 4 months, Sundays only, 18 sessions of 3 hours each
  • Weekly: 2.5 months, 30 sessions of 3 hours each (3 times a week)

Advance Beauty Course

₹44,999

Applicable taxes extra, if any.

Eligibility

For learners interested in advanced beauty and makeup techniques. Prior experience in beauty treatments is required. No academic education requirement is specified.

Course syllabus

  • Advance manicure: American, Shellac, Dip Powder, Acrylic
  • Advance pedicure: Hot Stone, Salt, Sports, Margarita
  • Advance facials: Skin Tightening, Shine & Glow, Thermogenic Mask, Oxy Blast, Skin Whitening, Gold, Pearl, Silver, Diamond and Bridal Facial
  • Galvanic, High Frequency, Ultrasonic and Iontophoresis machine usage
  • Advance waxing: Soft, Hard, Chocolate, Fruit
  • Advance makeup: Dramatic Night, Red Lip & Golden Eyes, Pop of Color, Smudged & Nude Base
  • Derma abrasion techniques and pimple treatments
  • Revision and examination

Duration options

  • Sunday batch: 4.5 months, 21 sessions of 3 hours each
  • Weekly batch: 3 months, 36 sessions of 3 hours each
